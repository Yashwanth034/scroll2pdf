# Privacy disclosure

Scroll2PDF processes page pixels and DOM geometry locally to scroll, crop, stitch, and paginate user-requested captures. It does not transmit data to a server, use analytics or telemetry, read cookies or authentication tokens, call external OCR/services, or call WhatsApp/Telegram private APIs. Temporary results are stored locally in IndexedDB only for the result/download flow and are cleaned after the result page opens or expires.
